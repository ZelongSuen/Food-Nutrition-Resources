Food & Nutrition Resources

CalFresh Application · Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP)

Apply Now: CalFresh — Monthly food assistance benefits to purchase groceries and fresh produce for eligible students and households.

Visit the Berkeley City College Basic Needs Center during open hours for direct, one-on-one assistance with your application, eligibility evaluation, and required verification documents. Online applications can also be submitted directly through BenefitsCal.com.
Campus Support: BCC Basic Needs Center State Portal: BenefitsCal.com Coverage: Fresh Grocery & Market Matching
To search for the pantry or distribution site closest to your current location across Alameda County, visit the ACCFB search tool at foodnow.net/find-a-food-pantry/ to find groceries, cold meal packages, hot meal services, and CalFresh enrollment hubs.
Note: If you are seeking immediate on-campus provisions, visit the BCC Basic Needs Center Pantry during official operating hours. All off-campus community resources listed below operate independently.

Online Resources & Benefit Support

Official regional locators, benefit match programs, and digital state nutrition application portals.

County Directory Food Now (ACCFB Online Food Locator System) ↗
Online Platform

24/7 search tool for free food pantries, hot meal distributions, and mobile pantry trucks open on the current day across Alameda County.

Intake Guide: The primary underlying directory run by the Alameda County Community Food Bank (ACCFB); enter your ZIP code to filter the nearest active distribution sites by operating day.
Platform: foodnow.net Coverage: All Alameda County Cities
EBT Produce Match Market Match (California Farmers' Market EBT Match Program) ↗
Online Directory

Matches CalFresh EBT benefits dollar-for-dollar when purchasing fresh fruits and vegetables (typically provides a $10 free produce voucher match for spending $10).

How to Use: Swipe your CalFresh EBT card at the information booth of any of Berkeley's three farmers' markets (Downtown, North, South) to receive bonus market tokens/vouchers.
Directory: marketmatch.org Benefit: Up to $10 produce match per visit
State Registry California EBT Retailer Locator ↗
Online Tool

Official state search tool for locating authorized grocery stores, corner markets, supermarkets, and direct farm stands that accept CalFresh EBT card payments.

Portal: ebt.ca.gov/locator Scope: Statewide Retail Directory
Special Supplemental California WIC Program (Women, Infants, and Children) ↗
🗺️ Map ↗ Online & Center St

Provides supplemental nutritious food packages, nutrition education, and farmers' market nutrition coupons for low-income pregnant women, postpartum mothers, and families with infants or children under age five.

Online Portal: myfamily.wic.ca.gov Berkeley Local Office: 1947 Center St, Berkeley, CA

Regional Hubs (Alameda Island & San Leandro)

Dedicated island grocery marketplaces and south county comprehensive multi-service resource centers.

🗺️ Map ↗ Alameda Island

Large full-choice grocery distribution marketplace. Open Mondays & Fridays 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM; Wednesdays 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM.

Comprehensive Hub Davis Street Family Resource Center (Emergency Food Pantry) ↗
🗺️ Map ↗ San Leandro BART

Distribution of free fresh vegetables, fruits, staple groceries, and frozen items (open Monday through Thursday, 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM).
